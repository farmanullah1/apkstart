* Switches between phone and tablet layout.
* Key point: **never touch wm size**. Changing wm size alters the guest framebuffer dimensions
* (720x1280 -> 1280x720) while the embedded Qt window keeps its original size and orientation, so the picture is
* squeezed or rotated 90 degrees and mouse coordinates go wrong. Worse, the wm size override persists
* in /data and survives a reboot - that is the root of the repeated "black bars / not filled / clicks land nowhere"
* reports.
* Now only logical density changes (wm density), and the framebuffer stays 720x1280 upright:
*   - 320dpi -> smallestWidth 360dp -> phone layout
*   - 190dpi -> smallestWidth 606dp (>=600dp) -> tablet / split layout
* Size, orientation and touch mapping all stay the same, so nothing lies on its side, there are no black bars, and taps always match.
